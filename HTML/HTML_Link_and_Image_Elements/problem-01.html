GLA University Greater Noida

GLA University Greater Noida is a university campus located in Knowledge Park II, Greater Noida. It provides different undergraduate and postgraduate courses and focuses on academic learning as well as practical skills.

About:

GLA University has a learning environment where students can study different subjects and take part in activities outside the classroom. The Greater Noida campus is located at 15A, Knowledge Park II, Greater Noida .

The university also provides facilities for students to learn through projects, practical work and industry related activities.

Programs:

Some of the programs offered at the Greater Noida campus include B.Tech CSE, B.Tech CSE with Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, B.Tech Electronics and Computer Engineering, AI-Enabled BBA, AI-Enabled MBA and MCA with Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.

I am interested in the B.Tech CSE - Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning program because AI and machine learning are useful in many areas of technology.

Campus Life:

Campus life is not only about studies. Students can take part in sports, cultural activities and different events. Activities like cricket, volleyball, chess and other games can make college life more enjoyable.
