TechFest 2026

Technology, Ideas and Fun:

TechFest 2026 is a college technology festival where students can learn new things, take part in activities and show their technical skills. It is a good chance for students to meet others who are interested in technology.

Introduction:

TechFest is being organised for college students who are interested in computers, artificial intelligence, coding and new technology. Students can participate in different activities and enjoy a day full of learning and competition.

The main aim of the event is to encourage students to try something different and improve their practical knowledge.

Highlights:

Live coding activities and technology competitions will be the main attractions of TechFest 2026.

Students can also attend project demonstrations, take part in quizzes and interact with other participants during the event.

Date and Venue:

Registration Date:December 16-18, 2026
Time: Not Declared Yet
Venue: IIT Bombay, Mumbai, India

Registration closes on 10 October 2026.

Speakers:

The event will have guest speakers from the technology field. They will talk about current technology, career opportunities and the skills students should develop for the future.

Students will also get a chance to ask questions and learn from their experience.
