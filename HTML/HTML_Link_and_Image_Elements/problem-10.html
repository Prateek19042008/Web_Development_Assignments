About Institution:

GLA University Greater Noida is located in Knowledge Park II, Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh. The campus focuses on education, practical learning and industry exposure.

The Greater Noida campus offers courses like B.Tech CSE, B.Tech CSE-AIML and other programs.

Principal / Director Message:

The university encourages students to improve their knowledge and skills. Students are also given opportunities to work on projects and learn through practical activities.

Regular practice and hard work can help students improve.

Academic Programs:

GLA Greater Noida offers programs in different areas. Some programs include B.Tech CSE, B.Tech CSE-AIML, B.Tech Electronics and Computer Engineering, AI-Enabled BBA and other courses.
