Agra Travel Guide

About Agra:

Agra is a famous city in Uttar Pradesh. It is well known for the Taj Mahal and its rich history. Many tourists visit Agra every year.

Famous Attractions:

The Taj Mahal is the most famous attraction in Agra. Other popular places include Agra Fort and Mehtab Bagh.

Taj Mahal Official Website

Culture

Agra has a rich Mughal history and its culture is influenced by different traditions. The city is also known for its handicrafts and marble work.

Food:

Agra is famous for its local food. Petha is one of the most popular sweets of the city. Tourists can also enjoy different North Indian dishes.

Best Time to Visit:

The best time to visit Agra is during the cooler months. October to March is a good time because the weather is more comfortable for sightseeing.

Travel Tips:

Carry water and comfortable shoes when visiting tourist places. It is also better to start sightseeing early to avoid large crowds.
