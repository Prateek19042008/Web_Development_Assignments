Students Organize Clean Campus Drive

College students come together to keep their campus clean

College students organized a clean campus drive to spread awareness about cleanliness and keeping the surroundings neat. Many students took part in the activity.


The drive was held at the college campus in Greater Noida. Students cleaned different areas of the campus and collected waste from places where it had been thrown.


The activity was organized on August 29, 2026. Teachers also encouraged the students to take part and maintain cleanliness in their daily lives.


The main aim of the drive was to make students understand the importance of a clean environment. Students said that small efforts can make the campus a better place for everyone.
