About the Expo

The National Tech & Career Expo 2026 is a platform where students can meet companies, attend workshops and learn about new career opportunities.

The event will include technology companies, startup founders, trainers, recruiters and industry experts.

Entry is free for registered students and the event will provide many learning and networking opportunities.

Message from the Organizer

Welcome to the Expo

Our aim is to connect students with the technology industry and help them understand real career opportunities.

"Learn new skills, meet new people and take the next step towards your career."

Students should bring their college ID and a copy of their resume.

How to Prepare

  • Carry your college ID card.
  • Prepare an updated resume.
  • Research participating companies.
  • Prepare some questions for recruiters.
  • Wear comfortable and formal clothes.
  • Attend useful workshops.

Attendee Journey

  1. Complete online registration.
  2. Reach the expo venue.
  3. Collect your visitor pass.
  4. Visit company booths.
  5. Attend workshops and talks.
  6. Interact with recruiters.
  7. Explore internship and job opportunities.

Important Career Terms

Career Expo
An event where students meet companies and explore career opportunities.
Internship
A short work experience program for students.
Placement
A job opportunity offered to eligible students or graduates.
Workshop
A practical learning session conducted by an expert.
Certification
A document showing completion of a course or training.
Networking
Building professional connections with people and companies.
