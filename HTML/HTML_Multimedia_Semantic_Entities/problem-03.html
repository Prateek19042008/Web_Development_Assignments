Welcome to Uttarakhand

Uttarakhand is a beautiful state in northern India known for its mountains, rivers, temples, forests and adventure activities. It is also known as the Land of the Gods.

Visitors can enjoy trekking, rafting, camping, sightseeing and peaceful hill stations. The state offers something for both adventure lovers and people looking for a relaxing trip.

For official information, visit Uttarakhand Tourism .

You can also explore the official Uttarakhand Tourism YouTube channel .

Popular Attractions

Rishikesh

Rishikesh is famous for yoga, spirituality and adventure activities such as river rafting.

Nainital

Nainital is a popular hill station known for its beautiful lake and pleasant mountain surroundings.

Mussoorie

Mussoorie is a well-known hill station with mountain views, waterfalls and a pleasant climate.

Kedarnath

Kedarnath is an important pilgrimage destination located in the Himalayan region.

Valley of Flowers

Valley of Flowers is known for its beautiful natural scenery, flowers and trekking routes.

Auli

Auli is popular for mountain views, skiing and other adventure activities.

Jim Corbett National Park

Jim Corbett is famous for wildlife and nature experiences.

Haridwar

Haridwar is an important spiritual destination located on the banks of the River Ganga.

Things to Do

  • River rafting in Rishikesh
  • Trekking in the Himalayas
  • Camping in the mountains
  • Visit temples and spiritual places
  • Explore wildlife parks
  • Enjoy local food
  • Visit hill stations
  • Enjoy winter activities in Auli

One-Day Uttarakhand Trip Plan

  1. Start the morning with breakfast.
  2. Visit a nearby sightseeing location.
  3. Explore a local market.
  4. Have lunch and try local food.
  5. Visit a famous temple or natural attraction.
  6. Enjoy the evening view.
  7. Return to the hotel and take rest.

Travel Facts & Information

Uttarakhand has many natural and cultural attractions & is a good destination for different types of travellers.

A normal travel budget can be around ₹5,000 – ₹15,000 depending on the destination and number of days.

Temperatures can change quickly in mountain areas, so visitors should carry suitable clothes. A temperature of 15°C may feel quite different in the mountains.

Entry requirements can be different for different locations. Please check official information before travelling.
