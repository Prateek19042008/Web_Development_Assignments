Student terms and Rules

General Rules:

Students must follow the rules and regulations of the institution and behave respectfully with teachers, staff, and other students. They should keep the campus clean, carry their identity card, and follow classroom instructions. Every student is responsible for maintaining proper behaviour and discipline. Note: Students should always follow the instructions given by the college authorities.

Attendance:

Regular attendance is important for academic performance and participation in classroom activities. Students should attend classes on time and avoid unnecessary absences. Students must maintain at least 75% attendance to be eligible for the semester examination. Medical leave may require valid supporting documents.

examination:

Students must follow all examination rules and instructions given by the examination department. They should carry their identity card and required stationery to the examination hall. Students must reach the examination hall at least 15 minutes before the examination begins.Mobile phones, smart watches, books, and other unauthorized materials are not allowed inside the examination hall.

Assignment Submission:

Students must complete their assignments properly and submit them through the method specified by the teacher. Assignments should be original, well-presented, and submitted within the given time. All assignments must be submitted before the announced deadline. Late submission may result in marks being deducted or the assignment not being accepted.

Discipline:

Students must maintain discipline and respect others on the college campus. Fighting, bullying, damaging college property, using abusive language, and disturbing classes are strictly prohibited. Serious misconduct may result in disciplinary action. Students are expected to behave responsibly both inside and outside the classroom.

Important Notices:

Students should regularly check official college notices and announcements for updates about classes, examinations, assignments, and other activities. Students must check important notices regularly to avoid missing deadlines. Always verify important information through an official college source.

Important Deadlines:

Students should complete all academic work within the specified time. Assignment Submission: 31 August 2026.
Examination Registration: 5 September 2026.
Note: Contact the concerned teacher or department immediately if you have a genuine problem meeting a deadline

Submission Instructions:

Every assignment should include the student's name, enrollment number, course, semester, subject, and date of submission. Students should submit their work through the platform or method instructed by the teacher. Incomplete or incorrectly submitted assignments may not be accepted for evaluation.
