Chemistry:

Water is represented by the formula H2 Carbon dioxide is represented by CO2 Oxygen is represented by O2 Sulfuric acid is represented by H2SO4 Important: Chemical formulas use subscripts to show the number of atoms of each element.

Physics:

The area of a square is A = a2 The volume of a cube is V = a3 The famous equation of energy is E = mc2 Scientific expressions often use powers and mathematical symbols to represent physical quantities.

Biology:

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants prepare their food using sunlight, carbon dioxide, and water. The basic unit of life is the cell. Cells contain genetic material called DNA.
