Steps Before a Practical Class

  1. Read the practical experiment before coming to the lab.
  2. Bring the required notebook and lab material.
  3. Reach the laboratory on time.
  4. Listen to the instructions given by the faculty.
  5. Perform the practical carefully.
  6. Complete the observation and submit the work.

Academic Rules

  • Students must attend classes regularly.
  • Students must carry their college ID card.
  • Mobile phones should be kept away during lectures.
  • Students should maintain cleanliness in classrooms.
  • Assignments should be submitted before the given deadline.
  • Students should follow laboratory safety instructions.

Academic Terms

Lecture
A class where a teacher explains a particular topic.
Lab
A practical class where students perform experiments or programs.
Tutorial
A class mainly used for practice and solving questions.
Internal Assessment
Marks given during the semester through tests, assignments or other work.
Practical
A session in which students apply their theoretical knowledge.

Department Message
