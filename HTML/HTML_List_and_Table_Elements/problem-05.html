Annual College Fest 2026

Welcome to our Annual College Fest 2026. The fest is planned for three days and includes competitions, cultural activities, games and other events for students.

Fest Introduction

The college fest gives students a chance to participate in different activities and show their skills. Students can take part in individual as well as team events.

Registration Process

  1. Choose the event in which you want to participate.
  2. Open the registration form.
  3. Enter your name and college details.
  4. Select individual or team participation.
  5. Submit the required information.
  6. Save the registration/reference number.
  7. Report to the venue at the given time.

Open Registration Form

Fest Rules

  • Participants must carry their college ID card.
  • Participants must report before the event starts.
  • Students should follow the instructions of coordinators.
  • Any kind of unfair practice may lead to disqualification.
  • Students should maintain cleanliness at the event venues.
  • Participants should take care of their personal belongings.

Important Terms

Open Event
An event in which participants meeting the given conditions can take part.
Team Event
An event where two or more participants compete together as a team.
Individual Event
An event in which a participant competes alone.
Registration
The process of entering participant details for an event.
Coordinator
A person responsible for managing a particular event.
Final Round
The last stage of a competition where selected participants compete.
