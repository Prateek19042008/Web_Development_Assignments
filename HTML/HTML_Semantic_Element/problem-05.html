About the Career Fair

The National Career & Education Fair 2026 is an event for students, graduates and job seekers. It provides an opportunity to learn about different careers, courses, internships and job opportunities.

The main objective of the event is to help participants understand their career options and interact with organizations and education providers.

Event Date: 20 October 2026
Venue: India Expo Centre, Greater Noida

Objectives of the Event

  • Help students explore career options.
  • Provide information about higher education.
  • Connect job seekers with organizations.
  • Provide information about internships.
  • Give guidance about scholarships and placements.

Participating Organizations

Technology Companies

Technology organizations will provide information about software, data, artificial intelligence and other technical career opportunities.

Education Institutions

Education institutions will provide information about courses, admission requirements and higher education.

Preparation Guide

Participants should prepare before attending the event so they can make better use of their time.

  • Carry an updated resume.
  • Prepare a short introduction about yourself.
  • Research the organizations you are interested in.
  • Carry important educational details.
  • Prepare some questions to ask recruiters.
  • Dress appropriately for interviews.

Attendee Journey

  1. Register for the event.
  2. Reach the venue and complete entry.
  3. Attend the sessions of your choice.
  4. Meet organizations and ask questions.
  5. Submit applications if interested.
  6. Collect useful information before leaving.

Career Terms

Career Fair
An event where students and job seekers meet organizations and learn about opportunities.
Internship
A short work experience that helps students develop practical skills.
Placement
The process of helping students or candidates find suitable employment.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students for education.
Apprenticeship
A training opportunity where a person learns skills while gaining practical experience.
