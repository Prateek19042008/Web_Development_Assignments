How to Request an Appointment

  1. Select the required department.
  2. Choose a doctor and preferred date.
  3. Enter your contact details.
  4. Provide the reason for the visit.
  5. Submit the appointment request.

Common Terms

Consultation
A meeting with a healthcare professional for advice or evaluation.
Follow-up
A later visit to review the progress after an earlier consultation.
OPD
Outpatient Department where patients receive consultation without admission.
Appointment
A scheduled time for visiting a doctor or clinic.

Patient Instructions

  • Carry your appointment details.
  • Bring previous reports if required.
  • Reach the clinic on time.
  • Keep your contact information correct.
