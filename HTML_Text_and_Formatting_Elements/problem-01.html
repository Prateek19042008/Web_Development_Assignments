Campus life:

    

        Campus life at GLA University, Greater Noida is a vibrant mix of academics, fun, creativity, and personal
            growth. Students get opportunities to participate in cultural events, technical competitions, sports, clubs,
            workshops, and various college activities. The campus provides a friendly environment where students
        can make new friends, explore their interests, develop new skills, and gain confidence. For students from
        technical fields like CSE and AI-ML, the campus also offers opportunities to explore technology, coding,

        participate, compete, and create memorable moments.
